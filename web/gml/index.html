Read GML in your browser

Open or drop a GML (Geography Markup Language) file, a zip archive of them, or a response you saved from a WFS (Web Feature Service). Files ending in .gml or .xml both work. Nothing from this page is uploaded anywhere: xeibe runs in your browser.

You'll see the features in a table and on a map. You'll also see how xeibe read the file: the columns of each layer, the order of the coordinates and anything it had to skip.

To draw the map, coordinates are converted from the file's coordinate reference system (CRS) by an AI port of PROJ. Grids come from cdn.proj.org. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Select a feature on the map or a row in the Data tab to see its XML.
