xeibe GitHub

Demo page of the xeibe library

AI-generated library for reading GML files and converting coordinates. Nothing from this page is uploaded anywhere, everything runs in your browser.

Main purpose of this site is for easy validation of correctness of results.

Read GML

Open a GML (Geography Markup Language) file, a zip archive of them, or a response you saved from a WFS (Web Feature Service) in your browser.

Open the GML page →

Reproject coordinates

Paste coordinates and convert them between two coordinate reference systems (CRSs).

Open the reproject page →

Coordinates are converted by an AI port of PROJ. Verify correctness of results with more mature and tested libraries. Grids come from cdn.proj.org. Coordinate reference systems come from the EPSG (European Petroleum Survey Group) dataset, © IOGP (International Association of Oil & Gas Producers). Notices
