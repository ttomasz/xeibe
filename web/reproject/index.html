Input

Output

Operations

An operation is the chain of steps that takes coordinates from the source CRS to the target. Projecting coordinates is a matter of formulas and loses no accuracy. Changing the datum relies on a transformation published by EPSG, fitted to surveyed points in a particular area, either as a few parameters or as a grid of shifts. It is only as accurate as EPSG states, and only within that area. Between two datums there are often several such transformations, and their results differ.

Check with PROJ

Coordinates are converted by an AI port of PROJ. Verify correctness of results with more mature and tested libraries. Grids come from cdn.proj.org. EPSG data © IOGP, . Notices

Fetching grids

The transformations these points may go through shift coordinates by grids. Only the parts of each file around the points are fetched, from cdn.proj.org, and kept while the page is open.
